Quick Answer

A concrete driveway in Columbus, OH typically costs $4,000-$8,000 for a standard two-car driveway. Price depends on size (400-600 sq ft), thickness, finish, and site conditions. Stamped or colored concrete adds $2-4 per square foot. Columbus Concrete Guys offers free, detailed estimates.

Average Cost in Columbus

The average concrete driveway in Columbus costs $4,000-$8,000. A basic 2-car driveway (about 500 sq ft) with a broom finish runs $4,000-$5,500. A larger driveway, thicker slab, or decorative finish pushes it toward $7,000-$8,000. These are real Central Ohio prices, not national averages.

What Drives the Price

Size is the biggest factor — most 2-car driveways are 400-600 sq ft. Thickness matters too: residential driveways need 4-6 inches, but heavier vehicles need more. Site access, sub-base condition, and finish all affect cost. Removing an old driveway adds $500-$1,500.

Concrete vs. Asphalt

Concrete costs more upfront ($4,000-$8,000 vs. $3,000-$6,000 for asphalt) but lasts 25-30 years vs. 15-20 for asphalt. Concrete needs less maintenance and offers more design options. For Columbus homeowners staying 10+ years, concrete is the better value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 2-car concrete driveway cost in Columbus?

A standard 2-car concrete driveway in Columbus costs $4,000-$5,500 with a basic broom finish. Larger driveways or decorative finishes cost more.

How much does stamped concrete cost per square foot in Ohio?

Stamped concrete in Ohio costs $8-$15 per square foot installed, including labor and materials. It's more expensive than plain concrete but mimics stone, brick, or wood.

Is it cheaper to pave or concrete a driveway?

Asphalt is cheaper upfront ($3,000-$6,000) but concrete lasts longer (25-30 years vs. 15-20) and needs less maintenance. Over the long term, concrete is usually the better value.
