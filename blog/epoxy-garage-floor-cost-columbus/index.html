Average Cost in Columbus

Epoxy garage floors in Columbus cost $3-$8 per square foot. A standard 2-car garage (400-500 sq ft) runs $1,500-$4,000. Premium metallic or flake systems cost more. We give exact quotes after inspecting your floor.

What's Included

A quality epoxy job includes surface grinding, crack repair, primer, base coat, color flakes (optional), and 2-3 top coats. Cheap quotes often skip prep — that's why they peel. We do full prep for a floor that lasts 15-20 years.

DIY vs. Professional

DIY epoxy kits cost $100-$300 but rarely last more than a year or two in a garage. Professional installation costs more but includes proper prep and a warranty. For a floor you'll use daily, professional is worth it.
