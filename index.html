Frequently Asked Questions About Concrete in Columbus

How much does a concrete driveway cost in Columbus, OH?

A standard concrete driveway in Columbus typically costs between $4,000 and $8,000, depending on size, thickness, and finish options. Stamped concrete and decorative finishes add $2-4 per square foot. We provide free on-site estimates throughout the Columbus metro area.

How long does epoxy flooring last on a garage floor?

Professional epoxy garage floors typically last 15-20 years with proper preparation and installation. In Columbus's climate with freeze-thaw cycles, proper moisture barrier and primer coats are essential for longevity. Our epoxy installations include a lifetime warranty against peeling.

What's the best time of year to pour concrete in Ohio?

The best time for concrete work in Columbus is April through October when temperatures stay above 50°F. However, we can pour concrete year-round using cold-weather techniques like heated enclosures and accelerating admixtures. Spring and fall are ideal — moderate temperatures mean optimal curing conditions.

Do you offer free estimates in Columbus?

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation estimates for all concrete projects in Columbus and Central Ohio. Our estimator will visit your property, take measurements, discuss your options, and provide a detailed written quote — usually within 24-48 hours of your request.
